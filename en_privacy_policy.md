# Privacy Policy

**TAEHAERIN** (hereinafter referred to as the "Company") complies with the Personal Information Protection Act and relevant laws to protect users' freedom and rights, legally processing and safely managing personal information. The Company establishes and discloses this Privacy Policy to inform users of the procedures and standards for handling personal information and to handle related grievances promptly and smoothly.

**Article 1 (Purpose of Processing and Items Collected)**
1. 'Melody Sketch' (hereinafter referred to as the "App") operates entirely based on an offline local storage (SQLite) system.
2. The Company does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal information (such as name, phone number, email, or location data) to external servers.
3. All melodies and project data created while using the App are safely stored ONLY on the user's device.

**Article 2 (Provision of Personal Information to Third Parties)**
Since the Company does not collect users' personal information, there is no personal information to provide to third parties. However, if a user utilizes the 'Share' feature (e.g., MIDI export) within the App to transfer data to an external app, it is strictly governed by the user's device operating system (OS) and the policies of the external service chosen by the user.

**Article 3 (Procedures and Methods of Destroying Personal Information)**
Because all data generated in the App is stored only locally on the device, if the user uninstalls the App or resets the device, all data is immediately and permanently destroyed in an unrecoverable state.

**Article 4 (Rights and Obligations of Users and Legal Representatives)**
Since the Company does not collect or retain user personal information on a server, there is no target information applicable for exercising rights such as requests for access, correction, deletion, or suspension of processing.
